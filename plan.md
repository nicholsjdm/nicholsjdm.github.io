# Action plan

The mission stays the same after this capstone and this degree: protect agency and reduce harm. These are the goals on the horizon (as of October 2026).

## Professional

- I will submit my graduation application after I complete this class for my BS in Information Technology.
- Once it is approved, I will update my resume. Each application gets the portfolio URL and a sanitized incident-response case, not only the resume.
- I will keep applying to junior SOC analyst roles, remote or in Tennessee, so the job and my children are in the same state.
- After I move to Tennessee, I will prepare for and apply to the Tennessee Air National Guard as an officer candidate for cyberspace operations at McGhee Tyson Air National Guard Base in Knoxville. The aim is to serve, including against advanced persistent threats. Selection is not mine to promise.
- I will add a Home Lab Projects section to this site and document what I build after the degree.
- I will link this portfolio on LinkedIn once it is clean of client detail.
- I will study for the CompTIA PenTest+ by March 2027, and take the exam no later than June 2027 if the move slips the date.
- I will keep refining the playbook internally after supervisor review. I will test the scripts on Windows 11 and a Windows server, write the result in the test log, and not automate it in the RMM tool until I am authorized.

## Spiritual

- I will keep the three principles where I can see them at work and at home: truth over verdict, consecrated skill, power under covenant.
- On a hard ticket I isolate when one unknown foreign address is enough. I ask when I do not know. I do not read a keylog past what I need to say the data is sensitive.
- Scripture study and church attendance stay habits. Matthew 4 and “Thy will be done” are the lines I go back to when curiosity or pride shows up.

## People

The degree and the job are how I get closer to my children. I will keep the door open, keep contact steady, and rely on the Lord for the rest.

## How I will know it is working

- Resume updated with the BS in Information Technology from Ensign College.
- Interviews scheduled for junior SOC or MSSP roles.
- Air National Guard packet started, not a commission assumed.
- PenTest+ scheduled or passed by June 2027.
- Playbook used on a real ticket after review, with a shorter path than inventing the steps under stress.
- No client names and no live indicators on the public repo.
